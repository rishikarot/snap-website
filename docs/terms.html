4. Plans and billing

Pro costs US$9 per named user/month on monthly billing, or US$96 per named user/year on yearly billing. The yearly price is equivalent to US$8/month and is charged for the year. Applicable taxes are additional.

Pro includes 100 GB shared file storage per workspace. Extra 100 GB blocks cost US$5/month or US$48/year per workspace.

Early joining offer: US$80/user for the first year on yearly billing, renewing at US$96/user/year at current rates.

The calculator is an estimate, not a checkout. Before paying, review the final amount, billing period, renewal arrangements, cancellation process and any refund policy in the product checkout. This website does not invent or substitute those terms.
