Privacy Policy

How this website handles your information, and what to review before using the snap product.

1. Who we are

snap is a product of TechMind Software LLC.

Mashreq, Al Suq Al Kabeer Plot No. 29-0 Dubai, United Arab Emirates

For website privacy questions, contact business@techmindsoftware.com.

2. Information we collect

Registrations include company name, country, business type, administrator name and work email, plus optional phone and team size. Demo requests include your email and acknowledgement. Contact enquiries include your name, email address, enquiry type and message, plus any company name or phone number you choose to provide. We store a reference, submission date, enquiry status and email-notification status with each enquiry.

Hosting infrastructure also processes ordinary request information needed to deliver and protect the website. Request identifiers may be used to limit repeated submissions and sign-in attempts.

3. How we use it

We use enquiry information to respond to questions, assign follow-up, maintain a record of the conversation and protect the form from misuse. Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing.

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card information or confidential project records through the contact form.

4. Hosting and service providers

Website hosting and Supabase database services process information needed to operate the site. The selected database region is Singapore; other services may process information elsewhere. When transactional email is configured, the email provider receives information needed to notify our team and acknowledge your enquiry. These providers may process information outside your country.

Google Fonts may receive requests from your browser when fonts load. We do not claim a particular data-residency location or certification unless it is separately confirmed.

5. Cookies and browser storage

Staff sign-in uses Supabase Auth. An access token is kept for the current browser tab, removed on sign out and subject to the authentication service’s expiry. The browser walkthrough uses temporary sample data and resets on reload; it does not create product records.

The current marketing site does not include a marketing-analytics or advertising-tracking integration. If that changes, the notice and any required choices must be updated before the integration is enabled.

6. Retention and deletion

The normal administrative retention period for contact enquiries is 365 days, unless an ongoing relationship or a legal requirement calls for a different period. Authorised staff can delete records and purge enquiries past the configured period.

Product workspace data follows its separate retention rules. See demo and trial data timelines. Deleting a marketing enquiry does not automatically delete a product account.

7. Your requests and choices

Contact business@techmindsoftware.com to ask about access to, correction of or deletion of enquiry information. Include enough information to identify the enquiry, such as its reference. We may need to verify your identity before disclosing or changing records.

You can choose not to submit the form and instead explore the public product information and guided walkthrough.

8. Product accounts and Aigen

The website can direct you to separately configured demo, registration and login services. Before account activation, review the product-specific privacy information for workspace data, AI providers, processing purposes, access controls and retention.

Aigen may need to process prompts and relevant permitted work data through an AI provider to answer a request. Provider selection, contractual protections, processing locations and model-training arrangements must be disclosed by the product operator; this website does not make an unverified promise about those arrangements.

9. Additional product privacy information

Additional product privacy details will be provided before product account activation. For website enquiries, the handling described above applies.

10. Updates and contact

We may update this notice as website services change. The effective date appears above. For questions, contact business@techmindsoftware.com.
