Capture the work.

Start with a client request, an idea, or a site observation. Keep the original context in a note.

“Inspect Riverside, assign the team, and prepare the handover.”
02

Split it. Assign it. Move it.

Break the note into smaller tasks, give each one an owner, and follow its status.

03

Connect people and attendance.

Keep staff records with a monthly, daily, or hourly wage basis. Use attendance and recorded work to prepare wage figures for review.

Attendance + agreed wage basis a clear wage summary
04

Keep records in your own structure.

Use worksheets for issues, project references, checklists, or operational records that need more structure than a message.

One worksheet for site observations, another for project requests

Tag PO references to project costs. Keep commitments separate from actual expenses so the same spend is not counted twice.
