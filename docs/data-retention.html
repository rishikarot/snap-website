Know the next date

A trial with
a clear next step.

Demo access, trial expiry and paid subscriptions have different timelines. The connected product must show your exact dates.

Seven-day demo

Your server-backed demo workspace is active for 7 days from creation. Access links and verification codes expire after ten minutes; request new credentials to return while the workspace is still active. A new code does not restart the workspace clock. Use sample information, not business-critical records.

Fourteen-day registered Pro trial

After company registration and verification, the product activates a 14-day Pro trial. You can upgrade before it expires. You can also request extra time inside the product. An extension is not automatic: the product shows whether it was approved and the revised expiry date.

At trial expiry

If your workspace fits Free allowances, it can continue on Free. If a trial-only workspace exceeds those limits, its owner should receive 30 days of read/export access to upgrade, reduce usage, or export records. If no continuation is chosen, the trial-only workspace is scheduled for deletion after that grace period.

An existing Free workspace should not be deleted merely because its Pro trial ends. A Free owner may need to choose the one retained login user and reduce excess usage before write access resumes.

Reminders and permanent deletion

Show the expiry and deletion dates in the product. Send expiry reminders and further notices before deletion. Permanent project deletion has no undo. Backup ageing and operational data removal are separate processes and must follow the implemented retention schedule.

These are launch policy requirements for the connected product. Email notifications, extension approval, downgrade, read/export grace, purge scheduling and backup retention are not activated by this marketing website.
