Explore product capabilities, pricing, storage, Aigen, demo access and trial timing before deciding.

What is snap?+

snap is a simple business work OS connecting notes, project tasks, people, attendance, wage preparation, worksheets, invoices, purchase references and project profitability.

Who is snap designed for?+

snap is designed for software teams, field-service businesses, consultants, operations teams and other project-based businesses. You can also shape projects, worksheets and workflows around your own business requirements.

What can Aigen do?+

Use prompts to create project structures with task breakdowns and planned dates, set up recurring tasks, or ask about accessible projects, tasks, workloads, targets and recent activity. Answers depend on recorded data and your permissions.

Can I try the application with my own sample records?+

Request demo access with your email. Our team will share access details when your workspace is ready. Your 7-day period starts when the demo is activated. You can also explore the guided walkthrough immediately without registering.

Does a demo access code stay valid for 7 days?+

Access details are supplied by the demo service when your workspace is ready. The 7-day demo period starts at activation. Mobile availability and return sign-in follow the connected product authentication service.

What details are needed to register?+

Provide your company name, country, business type, full name and work email. Phone and the number of people needing access are optional. If you choose Other, describe your business. Registration is saved for review; product activation is a separate step.

How long is the registered trial?+

A registered and verified workspace starts with a 14-day Pro trial. You can upgrade at any time before expiry. You can request additional days inside the product; extensions are subject to approval and the revised date must be shown.

What does snap Pro cost?+

Pro costs US$9 per named user/month or US$96 per named user/year, equivalent to US$8/month. Save US$12 per user/year with yearly billing. Applicable taxes are additional. Early joining offer: US$80/user for the first year on yearly billing, renewing at US$96/user/year at current rates.

Are Pro projects and rows unlimited?+

Pro has no fixed cap on the number of projects or retained business rows for ordinary use. File-storage allowances, import limits, API rates and safeguards for performance still apply. This is not an unrestricted raw database hosting service.

What does Free include?+

Free includes one login user, core features within Free allowances, 5 projects, 10000 retained rows including soft-deleted rows, 500 MB file storage, 10 MB per file, 100 automation actions/workspace/month and 50 Aigen credits/workspace/month.

How much file storage is included with Pro?+

Each paid workspace includes 100 GB shared file storage, independent of user count. Additional 100 GB blocks cost US$5/month or US$48/year each. The included base pool has no separate storage charge.

Does the 100 GB include database records?+

No. The file pool covers uploaded files and attachments, such as photos, PDFs and spreadsheets. Task fields, worksheet rows, attendance and other application records are stored as database data and are outside that file allowance. Pro has no fixed row-count cap for ordinary business use.

What is the maximum uploaded file size?+

Free supports up to 10 MB per file; Pro supports up to 100 MB. Files must fit available workspace storage and supported file-type rules. Large files may have separate Aigen processing limits.

Are Pro automations unlimited?+

Pro includes 10000 successful automation actions per workspace/month. A workflow with several successful actions uses several actions. Rate limits and loop protection still apply; the plan is not advertised as unlimited automation execution.

How many Aigen credits are included?+

Free includes 50 Aigen credits/workspace/month. Pro includes 1000 credits/paid user/month, pooled across the workspace. Trial and demo include 200 and 50 credits respectively for their full periods. Credits measure processing, not message count, and included monthly credits do not roll over.

Do web and mobile access require separate seats?+

No. The charge is for a named person’s access to the workspace, not for each device or session. Supported mobile clients must use the same product account and authentication service. App-store release availability should be checked separately.

Do staff records count as paid users?+

A staff record used only for attendance or wage preparation is not a paid login user. A person who signs in to view or manage work needs their own user access.

What happens at trial expiry?+

Continue on Free if the workspace fits its allowances, or subscribe to Pro. An oversized trial-only workspace should receive 30 days of owner read/export access before scheduled deletion if no continuation is chosen. Existing Free records should not be deleted simply because a Pro feature trial ends. See the data-retention page for implementation status.

Can snap transfer wages or replace statutory accounting?+

snap supports operational attendance, wage preparation, simple invoicing and project profitability. It does not imply bank payouts, tax filing, or a full statutory payroll/accounting system.

Why do soft-deleted rows count on Free?+

Soft-deleted records remain stored and count until permanently purged. Restoring one does not count it twice. Permanently dropping a project has no undo; export what you need first.

Where do I log in or request trial access?+

Log in through https://auth.techmindsoftware.com. Use the registration page to submit company details for review, or request demo access. Submitting a form does not itself activate a product workspace.
