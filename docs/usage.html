your plan includes.

Core features stay simple. These allowances explain files, automation and Aigen usage.

Files and records

Free includes 500 MB of file storage, 10 MB per file, 5 projects and 10000 retained rows. Pro includes 100 GB of shared file storage per workspace and 100 MB per file.

Storage covers uploaded attachments, images and documents. Business records are managed in the database separately. Pro has no fixed project or retained-row count cap for normal business use; service safeguards prevent abuse and disruptive workloads.

Extra 100 GB blocks cost $5/month or $48/year. Soft-deleted rows remain retained; permanently dropping a project cannot be undone.

Automation actions

Free: 100 successful actions/workspace/month. Pro: 10000 successful actions/workspace/month. A workflow with three successful actions uses three actions. An ordinary scheduled execution does not consume Aigen credits unless it calls Aigen.

Aigen credits

Free includes 50 credits/workspace/month. Pro includes 1000 credits/paid user/month, pooled across the workspace. A 14-day trial includes 200 credits in total; a 7-day demo includes 50 credits in total. Trial/demo allowances do not stack with recurring monthly allowances.

Credits measure AI processing, not messages. Longer context, larger project plans and multiple model calls consume more. Baseline text processing uses one credit per 1,000 input tokens or about 167 output tokens, or a mixture. Higher-cost models or paid AI tools must show their credit rate before use.

Input includes instructions, permitted work data, conversation context and tool results sent to the model. Billable reasoning output is counted. Included credits renew monthly without rollover. Aigen pauses at the limit; ordinary snap features remain available. Administrators can see usage. No automatic overage charges.

Aigen helps plan work and retrieve permitted records. Review dates, assignments, calculations and proposed changes before relying on them. Image generation, voice and paid web research are not included in the standard launch allowance.

Access and trial

One paid seat is for one named person, across supported web and mobile clients. Staff records without login access are not paid seats. Registered workspaces begin a 14-day Pro trial. Extension requests are reviewed inside the product.
