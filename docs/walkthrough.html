Different wage bases. One clear view.

Change a wage basis, rate, attendance, or hours to see the labour estimate update. These sample figures represent one working day.

Monthly rate ÷ selected working days × attendance. Daily rate × attendance. Hourly rate × hours actually worked. Attendance is shown separately for hourly staff; entered hours determine their wage estimate.
