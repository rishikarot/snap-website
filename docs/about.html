Why snap

Built to make everyday
business work simpler.

snap began development in 2025 with a clear goal: help people turn ideas into organised work and deliver projects with greater confidence.

From scattered work to a connected day.

Notes, task lists, field updates, attendance and project figures often live in different tools and conversations. snap brings those everyday pieces into a simpler flow—from the first request to tasks, records, invoices and project costs.

With Aigen, you can also describe the project you want to start or ask about the work you are permitted to access.

Built on practical delivery experience.

snap (Simple Notes to Actionable Plans) is a product of TechMind Software LLC, established in 2023 and headquartered in Dubai, UAE. Its development is backed by more than 15 years of enterprise software delivery experience through LetMeDoit Technologies, our Indian entity founded in 2009.

Our delivery experience includes large-scale telecom field-force solutions serving 40,000+ users globally, with 500+ operational modules across web and mobile. These systems handle 8–10 GB of daily file and image transactions and connect with 12+ enterprise applications.

That experience in scalability, enterprise integrations, complex workflows and real-world field operations shapes snap: practical capabilities brought together in a simple, intuitive work management platform.

These figures describe our enterprise delivery portfolio, separate from snap adoption and product usage.

One purpose. Clearer work.

Whether you lead a software team, coordinate site work, manage service projects or run daily operations, snap is designed to help you understand what is happening and what needs to happen next.
